About this map

Every state and Union Territory is drawn in its own bright colour. The colours only help you tell neighbours apart.

Drag to move, scroll or pinch to zoom. Zoom in to reveal more towns. Stars mark capitals and dots mark cities.

The map shows about 200 well-known cities and towns, not every one. Population figures are rounded from Census 2011 and areas are approximate. Borders are simplified and shown for learning.
